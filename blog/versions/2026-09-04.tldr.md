# September 4, 2026

A seven-day read-only audit found four lab systems reporting healthy while doing nothing: a seedbox that every dashboard showed 82% free while its real quota sat at 99.9% full, a retention tool that had deleted zero bytes in 13 days, and a scheduler that ran nothing for a week, all with green health checks the entire time.
